<p>In celebration of International Women&#8217;s Month and the 2026 theme #GiveToGain, JFrog hosted a virtual fireside chat on March 19, 2026: <a href="https://jfrog.com/webinar/women-in-devsecops-leveraging-ai-in-the-software-supply-delivery-lifecycle/">Women in DevSecOps: Leveraging AI in the Software Delivery Lifecycle</a>. Moderated by Shubha Gururaja Rao, Director of Solution Engineering at JFrog, the panel brought together two trailblazing technical leaders — Christine Tran, Head of Enterprise Platforms at Invesco, and Nhi Lam, Senior Software Engineering Manager at Vanguard — for a candid conversation at the intersection of AI, security, and culture.</p>
<p>Here are the key themes from the discussion.</p>
<h2>AI is No Longer Experimental</h2>
<p>The panel opened with a clear declaration: AI has moved well past the hype phase and is now a core architect of the modern software supply chain. The real question is no longer whether to integrate AI, but how to do it securely and responsibly.</p>
<p>Nhi Lam drove home the point that end-to-end traceability and provenance for all data and models within your artifacts is a non-negotiable requirement for true AI readiness. The panelists agreed that <a href="https://jfrog.com/learn/devops/artifact-management/">artifact management</a> has become a foundational pillar; enterprises must govern and secure the data fueling their AI models with the same rigor they apply to standard code. Without that foundation, AI integration introduces more risk than it removes.</p>
<h2>Solving the Productivity Paradox</h2>
<p>One of the most energizing parts of the conversation centered on what the panelists called the &#8220;productivity paradox,” or the frustrating reality that tools designed to help developers can actually slow them down when poorly implemented.</p>
<p>The solution, the panel agreed, starts with ruthlessly removing friction from daily workflows. Nhi pointed to context switching as a drain on developer focus, advocating for bringing security feedback and tooling directly into the IDE so engineers can stay in flow. The goal isn&#8217;t just better developer experience (DevEx) as an abstract concept, it&#8217;s returning joy to a developer by automating the mundane toil and freeing people up for the creative, high-value work they actually want to do.</p>
<h2>Less Is More</h2>
<p>As AI and security solutions have multiplied, tool sprawl has quietly become a major productivity killer. Christine and Nhi both emphasized that consolidating tools into a &#8220;single pane of glass&#8221; isn&#8217;t just a nice-to-have, but a strategic necessity.</p>
<p>Thoughtful <a href="https://jfrog.com/tool-consolidation/">tool consolidation</a> reduces technical debt, improves security posture, and makes it easier to manage costs. The challenge for technical leaders, the panel acknowledged, is balancing the constant pull of innovation against the very real need to simplify and secure the tech stack. More tools doesn&#8217;t necessarily mean more safety; often, it just means more noise.</p>
<h2>Shift Left as a Security Imperative</h2>
<p>Security in the era of AI-driven IDEs and automated pipelines demands a proactive, embedded approach. The panelists were emphatic: security can no longer be a checkbox tacked onto the end of a sprint.</p>
<p>Instead, organizations need to empower developers to take ownership of security early in the supply chain, and give them the real-time, actionable feedback they need to do it effectively. Shift-left security only works when developers have the right tools and the right context at the right moment.</p>
<h2>Leading by Example</h2>
<p>When Shubha asked the panel how women in DevSecOps can create more equitable pathways for the next generation, Nhi Lam shared a story. &#8220;I was meeting with a mentee and she shared that seeing me become a senior architect gave her the confidence that one day she could do that too,&#8221; Lam said. For her, leading by example means showing that you can move fluidly between engineering leadership and individual contribution, all while balancing the demands of learning new technologies like AI and taking care of a family.</p>
<p>As we celebrate International Women&#8217;s Month, events like this one serve as a reminder that representation in tech isn’t just symbolic, it’s transformational. The more women we see leading, building, and mentoring in DevSecOps, the more women will believe they belong here too.</p>
