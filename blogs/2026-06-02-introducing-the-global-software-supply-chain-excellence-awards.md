JFrog has launched the Software Supply Chain Excellence Awards, its inaugural customer recognition program celebrating teams excelling in software delivery across six categories: Convergence, Guardian, Trusted Intelligence, Control Plane, Elevation, and Legacy. The categories recognize achievements in tooling unification, security, AI governance, DevOps scaling, continuous learning, and societal impact. Nominations close July 17, 2026, with winners receiving trophies, press coverage, and ambassador status.
