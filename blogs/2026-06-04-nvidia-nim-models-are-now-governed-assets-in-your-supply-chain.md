JFrog's AI Catalog now manages NVIDIA NIM (Inference Microservices) models as governed supply chain assets, applying the same security controls previously limited to Docker images and software packages. Three-quarters of enterprises plan agentic AI deployment within a year, yet most pull NIM models directly from NVIDIA's registry, bypassing organizational governance policies and audit trails. The solution provides unified discovery, explicit allow/block policies, and complete version tracking for AI models alongside traditional artifacts in a single JFrog platform.
